MACH1 AIOS

A personal assistant app used by one person: its owner.

MACH1 AIOS is a private, personal productivity tool. It runs on its owner's own computer and helps him plan his day, keep track of school deadlines and sort his own email.

It connects to the owner's own Google account (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Tasks) with his permission. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
