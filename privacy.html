Privacy policy

MACH1 AIOS · Last updated October 6, 2026

MACH1 AIOS is a personal app used only by its owner. This policy explains how it handles Google user data.

  • Who uses it: only the owner, with his own Google account. No one else can sign in or use it.
  • What it accesses: the owner's own Gmail, Google Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Tasks, through Google's official APIs, only after he grants permission.
  • How data is used: to show the owner his own email, schedule, files and tasks, and to make changes he approves (for example calendar events or email drafts).
  • Where data lives: on the owner's own computer. Login tokens are stored encrypted on that computer.
  • Sharing: data is never sold, rented or shared with third parties, and never used for advertising.
  • Limited Use: use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Removing access: the owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
